# Contributing to Planet BSD

## Suggest a feed

Anyone can open an issue to suggest a feed; you don’t have to be the author.
Include the blog URL, its RSS or Atom URL, and a BSD post you think others
would enjoy. Please suggest the original author’s or project’s feed, not a
mirror or an aggregator.

Planet BSD is for writing about the BSDs. Technical opinions and disagreements
are welcome. Posts that mix BSD coverage with unrelated political or
ideological advocacy are out of scope.

If you write about several subjects, a BSD tag or category feed is enough. A
dedicated blog or subdomain works too. We review the posts that would appear
on Planet BSD, not everything you publish elsewhere.

Readers of any age should be able to follow a link from the feed. Please leave
out posts containing sexually explicit imagery, gore, or graphic depictions of
violence.
